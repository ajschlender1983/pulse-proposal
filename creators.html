What hosting a day asks of you

01 · Record

One short opening

A 2–5 minute audio or video piece opening your day's theme — in your own voice, your own style. Already have a piece that fits? We can feature existing work instead.

02 · Prompt

Two questions + one intention

A morning question, an evening question, and your signature intention line for the day. We draft, you approve — always in your voice, never ours.

03 · Share

Tell your people

Post your day to your own channels — announcement, reminder, last call. Your links carry your code; everything your audience does is attributed to you.

The deal · performance-based · no fluff
  • $100 per ring sold through your link or code — flat, per sale.
  • Plus commission on attributed revenue, paid monthly on close.
  • Dedicated tracking — your own code (YOURNAME100, worth $100 off for your audience), your own dashboard, transparent numbers.
  • No exclusivity, no content fees, no retainers — you keep your voice, your audience, and your other partnerships.
  • Total time ask: about an hour. Record once, approve the drafts, post three times.
