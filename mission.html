Brand & assets.

Joyce's guide is law. The campaign layer sits on top. Status tags show freshness at a glance.

Position have

Internal: "We don't sell a mindfulness ring. We sell the interruption." External, always: "Break autopilot." Buyer: the Overstimulated Striver. Master promise: interrupt autopilot before the day runs away with you — so the life on the other side of the pulse can actually reach you.

The word list have

Use — words that win

reset · pause · cue · interrupt · autopilot · overstimulated · attention · load · noise · presence · come back · downshift · one breath · screen-free · real life · no screens · no tracking · no dashboards · no data

Forbidden — never lead with

mindfulness (consumer lead) · meditation · wellness · zen · self-care jargon · "smart ring" · clinical claims · "a ring that vibrates" · spec comparisons to Oura / WHOOP / Apple Watch

WhatsApp · community engine
  1. One group, one purpose — the campaign home. Pin: current-day message + progress bar screenshot (real numbers only).
  2. Cadence rule — max 3 posts/day from Pulse: morning open, midday nudge, evening close. Community posts unlimited.

  4. Referral wiring — every member gets a personal code (their SubID) in a welcome DM; shares are attributed, always.

  6. Moderation — no medical claims, no forbidden-word marketing from Pulse's own account, kindness enforced quietly.
  7. Anti-doomscroll — the group models the product: evening close message ends with "go live your evening."

The model.

Why this campaign is shaped like this. Five panels, plain English. Full financial detail in the proposal.

1 · The problem with buying customers

At today's ad performance, Pulse loses money on every ad-bought ring. Spending more of the same is the "lose a month of runway" path.

2 · Anchor events flip the motivation

"Get the ring before the moment" beats "buy a ring" — the eclipse, the dance floor, and Unified Pulse give people a reason to order now.

3 · Partners are paid on results only

Creators and orgs earn per sale through their own tracked codes — no upfront fees, no wasted reach. Their audiences hear it in their voice, not ours.

4 · Honest scarcity does the selling

1,500 rings, real count, live progress bar. When it's gone it's gone until the next batch. No fake urgency anywhere, ever.

5 · Sold-out is the fundraise

If the batch clears, the next raise buys inventory against proven demand — a completely different conversation than raising for hoped-for growth.

Help.

The plain-English operating manual. New here? Read this page, then the Dashboard tells you what's next.

  1. What's a SubID? A tracking tag on every partner link so each sale credits the right person. It's how creators get paid and how we know what's working.
  2. Why is the progress bar "real numbers only"? Trust is the brand. A faked counter would cost more than it converts. The bar reads live Shopify inventory or it doesn't ship.
  3. How does the Unified Pulse Map window work? Each day opens 7am and closes 10pm in the participant's own time zone. Outside the window, the day rests. It's an anti-doomscroll product — the design says "go live your life."
  4. Why test at the eclipse before September? The Aug 10 send is a small, real A/B on our own list. It gives Elliot conversion data before a single big-audience dollar is spent.
  5. What if we don't hit 1,500? The proposal prices that honestly: worst case loses about what ads would have lost, and every asset (community, platform, film, data) survives into the next attempt.
  6. Who approves copy? Pulse-voice copy: the word-list check + Joyce. Partner-voice copy: the partner, always, before it ships.
  7. Where do decisions live? The Dashboard's "Awaiting your decision" panel. If it's not there, it's not blocking.
